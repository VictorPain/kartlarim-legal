Account and Data Deletion — Kartlarim

App: Kartlarim — Personal Finance Tracker Package name: com.fatihaci.kartlarim.app Developer: Fatih Acı — fatihaci79@gmail.com

This page exists so that you can delete your account and data without having to open the app, as required by Google Play's data deletion policy.

Option 1 — From inside the app (immediate)

  1. Open Kartlarim
  2. Go to the Profile tab
  3. Tap "Delete Account"
  4. Confirm both confirmation screens

This immediately and irreversibly deletes:

  • All financial data on your device — cards, debts, loans, incomes, fixed expenses, payment history, deferrals
  • Your identity record (email, name, user ID) held in Google Firebase Authentication

No waiting period applies.

Option 2 — Request by email

If you cannot install or open the app, send your deletion request to fatihaci79@gmail.com. Stating the email address you registered with is enough to identify your account.

Your request is handled free of charge within 30 days and the outcome is sent back to the same address.

What is not deleted, and retention periods

Data What happens
Financial data on your device Deleted immediately and permanently
Identity record (email, name, user ID) Deleted immediately and permanently
Backup files you sent to your own cloud Not deleted — these files are under your control and we hold no copy; delete them yourself from the service you used (iCloud, Drive, etc.)
Advertising ID logs (AdMob) Auto-deleted within ~60 days per Google's retention policy
Payment and subscription records (Apple / Google) Kept by the store for 10 years under tax and audit obligations; these records are not held by us

If you have a subscription — cancel it first

Deleting your account does not end your subscription. Your premium subscription is held on the Apple or Google Play side; to stop being billed you must cancel it separately from your store account settings. We cannot cancel it on your behalf.

  • iPhone / iPad: Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions
  • Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions
