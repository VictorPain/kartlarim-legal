This notice is prepared pursuant to Article 10 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK").

Architecture Note: Kartlarim is a local-first application. Your financial data (cards, debts, payments, expenses) is stored only on your device; it is not stored on any server we operate. Backups go to a cloud service of your choice — we do not retain, view, or have access to them.

1.1. Data NOT Processed by Us

The following data is never processed, stored, or viewed by us:

  • Your financial data — card limits, debts, payment history, fixed expenses, loans, deferrals: stored only in your device's local SQLite database
  • Your backup files — the encrypted ZIP files you create and upload to your own cloud (Section 6)
  • Bank passwords, bank API access credentials
  • Full credit card number (only the last 4 digits as an optional visual label)
  • CVC or card security code
  • Bank payment / transfer authorization information
  • Location data
  • Contacts, photos, files, media, microphone (except for Smart Scan — which runs instantly on user trigger and does not save images)

Snap-Sync Philosophy: Kartlarim does not connect directly to any bank. The user manually enters numbers from their bank app (snapshot). Therefore, Open Banking regulations are not triggered for our application. We don't connect to your bank, and we don't keep your financial data on our servers.

Legal Basis:

  • KVKK Art. 5/2-f (legitimate interest): Error tracking (Crashlytics), usage analytics (Analytics) — processing that does not harm the fundamental rights of the user, aimed at improving app quality.

4. Your Rights as a Data Subject (KVKK Article 11)

Under Article 11 of KVKK, you have the right to:

  • Learn whether your personal data is being processed
  • Request information about the processing
  • Learn the purpose of processing and whether it is being used appropriately
  • Know the third parties to whom data has been transferred (domestic or international)
  • Request correction, deletion, or destruction of the data
  • Request that correction/deletion be communicated to recipients of the data
  • Object to decisions made through automated analysis that adversely affect you
  • Claim compensation for damage arising from unlawful processing

In-app self-service methods:

  • Correction / access: Since your financial data is stored on your device, you can edit and view it within the app at any time.
  • Data download (KVKK Art. 11): From Profile → "Download My Data (KVKK)" you can download all data on your device in JSON format. This service is available to both free and premium members.
  • Deletion (KVKK Art. 7): From Profile → "Delete Account" you can permanently delete your account and data (see Section 5).

Email application: fatihaci79@gmail.com. Applications are answered free of charge within 30 days in accordance with the Data Controller Application Notification.

5. Account Deletion and Data Destruction

From Profile → "Delete Account" you can delete your account instantly. This action cannot be undone. When confirmed:

  • All financial data on your device (cards, debts, payments, expenses) is permanently deleted
  • Your Firebase identity record (email, UID) is deleted
  • Your RevenueCat subscription record (anonymized) is terminated
  • Crashlytics / Analytics anonymous data is auto-deleted at the end of those services' retention period (~60-90 days)

No 30-day waiting period applies; data is permanently destroyed. To prevent accidental deletion, the app requires a double "ARE YOU SURE?" confirmation.

If you have an active premium subscription: Cancel your subscription from your App Store / Google Play account settings before deleting the account. Otherwise the subscription will continue on Apple/Google's side.

5.1. Data Retention Periods

  • Device-side financial data: Until the user deletes it or removes the app from the device
  • Firebase Auth (email, UID): Until account deletion
  • RevenueCat subscription metadata: Subscription period + audit period (per RC retention policy)
  • Firebase Crashlytics: ~90 days (Firebase default)
  • Firebase Analytics: ~14 months (Firebase default, can be shortened from settings)
  • AdMob ad ID logs: ~60 days (AdMob default)
  • Premium payment metadata: 10 years (per tax and subscription audit obligation, on Apple/Google's side)

6. Backup (All Users — User-Controlled)

Backup does not require premium. All users (free + premium) can manually create backup files from Profile → "Backup".

Backup flow:

  1. User taps the backup creation button
  2. The app encrypts on the device using the passphrase you set (AES-256-GCM, PBKDF2 key derivation with 100,000 iterations)
  3. The encrypted ZIP file is created in the device's temporary cache
  4. The device's "Share Sheet" opens; you send the backup wherever you choose: iCloud Drive, Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, email, messaging app, etc.
  5. The file is uploaded to the service you chose

The Data Controller's role: Creation and encryption of backup files happen on your device. The service you share the backup with (iCloud Drive, Google Drive, Dropbox, email, messaging, etc.) is entirely your choice and those providers are subject to their own privacy policies. The Data Controller cannot access, view, copy, or take responsibility for the backup files stored in those services.

Restore: When moving to a new device or in case of data loss, the user downloads the backup from wherever it is stored and restores it through the in-app "Restore from Backup" menu by entering the passphrase.

⚠️ IMPORTANT — Backup Passphrase Responsibility: Your backup encryption passphrase is not transmitted to our servers, not known by us, not stored by us, and cannot be recovered under any circumstances. If the passphrase is lost, the backup file cannot be opened and its contents become inaccessible. The Data Controller cannot be held responsible for data loss arising from this situation. We recommend storing the passphrase in a secure password manager (1Password, Bitwarden, etc.) or in a physically secure location.

7. Data Security Measures (KVKK Art. 12)

  • Local storage: SQLite on device; sensitive tokens protected with iOS Keychain / Android EncryptedSharedPreferences
  • Backup encryption: AES-256-GCM + PBKDF2 (100,000 iterations) — based on user passphrase; the key is not on our side
  • Transit security: Firebase / RevenueCat / Apple / Google calls go over TLS 1.3 (HTTPS)
  • Server access: We do not operate any server; the security standards of third-party services (Firebase, RevenueCat) apply
  • Data breach notification: Per KVKK Art. 12/5, a discovered data breach is reported to the Authority and affected data subjects within 72 hours
