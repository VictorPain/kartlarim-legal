Privacy Policy — Kartlarim

Developer: Fatih Acı
Contact: fatihaci79@gmail.com
Effective Date: 16 August 2026 (v3)

This Privacy Policy explains the information collected when you use the Kartlarim mobile application ("App").

Local-first architecture: Your financial data (cards, debts, payments, expenses) is stored only on your device; it is not stored on any server we operate. If you create a backup, the file goes to a cloud of your choice (iCloud, Drive, etc.) — we do not retain it.

1. Information We Collect

Sign-in Methods (Firebase Auth): There are three ways to use the app:

  • As a guest — no account is created and no identity data is collected.
  • Google sign-in — Google shares your name and email address.
  • Email and password registration — your email address and password are sent to Firebase Auth. We never see your password; Firebase Auth stores it hashed.

This information is used only for identity verification.

Financial Data You Enter in the App: Kartlarim stores data such as card limit / available limit / debt, loan amount / installment, fixed expense, income, deferral and payment history. This data is stored only in the private application area on your device — it is not stored on or sent to any server we operate.

Snap-Sync Philosophy: Kartlarim does not connect directly to any bank; bank passwords, card numbers, CVC, or open banking API access are NEVER requested. The user manually enters values like "available limit" from their bank app. We don't connect to your bank, and we don't keep your financial data on our servers.

Subscription Information (RevenueCat): Your premium subscription status is held by RevenueCat. Only your Firebase UID (anonymous user ID) and subscription status are sent to RevenueCat; name, email, and financial data are not sent.

Technical Data: Kartlarim does not use Firebase Crashlytics or Firebase Analytics; no crash report or usage statistic is sent to us. Anonymous crash reports exist only through Apple's and Google's own operating-system level mechanisms, subject to your device settings.

Interface Images: To generate your profile avatar, the initials of your display name are sent to ui-avatars.com; to show the logo of a subscription service you track, that service's domain (e.g. netflix.com) is sent to Google's favicon service. No other data is included in these requests.

Ad Data (AdMob + AppLovin): AdMob infrastructure (including AppLovin mediation) is used for ad display on the free plan. Your device's anonymous ad ID is sent to these services; with consent, personalized ads are shown, otherwise non-personalized ads. When premium is active, ads are turned off entirely and the ad ID is not processed.

Premium Payment Information: Premium subscription payments go through Google Play or App Store infrastructure. Your payment card or bank information is not transmitted to us.

2. Information We Don't Collect

  • Bank password / API access
  • Full card number (only the last 4 digits as an optional visual label)
  • CVC or card security code
  • Location data
  • Microphone / camera (except Smart Scan — runs only on user trigger and does not save images)
  • Contacts access
  • Files and media on your device
  • Copy of financial data (stays on your device — not on our side)
  • Backup files (with you and in the cloud you choose — not on our side)

3. Purpose of Information Use

  • To perform the app's core financial tracking function
  • To verify premium subscription status and to gate premium features
  • To show ads (on free plan, with consent for personalization)
  • To display the profile avatar and subscription logos

4. Sharing with Third Parties (Including International Transfer)

The following service providers' servers are located internationally (USA/EU). Some of your data is transferred to these providers as mandatory infrastructure to operate the app. As no adequacy decision of the Turkish Data Protection Board exists for the destination countries, transfers are made on the basis of the standard contract under KVKK Art. 9/2-a, and executed contracts are notified to the Board.

Your personal data is not shared with third parties for commercial purposes and is not sold.

5. Data Security

  • On device: Data is kept only in the private application area (iOS UserDefaults / Android SharedPreferences and the app's own web storage); other apps cannot reach it and the operating system's device encryption (iOS Data Protection / Android File-Based Encryption) applies. The app does not add its own encryption layer on top of this data
  • Backup encryption: AES-256-GCM with user passphrase (PBKDF2, 100,000 iterations) — passphrase is not on our side
  • In transit: TLS 1.3 (HTTPS) — Firebase, RevenueCat, Apple, Google calls
  • Data breach notification: Per KVKK Art. 12/5, within 72 hours to the Authority and affected data subjects

6. Backup (All Users)

Backup is available to all users (no premium required). From Profile → "Backup" you can create backup files. The backup is created on your device, encrypted with your chosen passphrase (AES-256-GCM), then the device's Share Sheet opens; you send the backup to the service of your choice (iCloud Drive, Google Drive, email, etc.).

We do not retain, view, or have access to your backup files. The privacy policy of the service you share to (Apple, Google, Dropbox, etc.) applies.

⚠️ Backup passphrase: The passphrase is not transmitted to our servers and cannot be recovered. If you lose the passphrase, the backup file cannot be opened. Store your passphrase in a secure place.

7. Children's Privacy

Kartlarim is not directed at children under 13. The application is a financial tool for adult users aged 18+.

8. Deleting Your Data

  • Removing the app from your device: All locally stored financial data is permanently deleted.
  • Account deletion (Profile → "Delete Account"): Instantly and permanently deleted — all financial data on your device + Firebase identity record + RevenueCat subscription record. No 30-day waiting period; cannot be undone.
  • Download my data (KVKK Art. 11): From Profile → "Download My Data (KVKK)" you can take your device data in JSON format. Free and premium users.
  • Email application: fatihaci79@gmail.com — answered within 30 days.

9. Changes to This Policy

If the policy changes, this page is updated and the "Last Updated" date is changed. For significant changes, the in-app data notice consent is requested again (versioning mechanism).
